Welcome aboard.
Let's get you found.

Four quick steps, about 10 minutes in total. You never need to share your Facebook, Instagram or Google password with us, and your accounts always stay yours.

  1. 2 min

    Facebook and Instagram: tap "Approve"

    We send an access request from our HeyFoundly business account to your Facebook Page and Instagram. You get a notification. Open it and tap Approve. That's it.

  2. 2 min

    Google: add us as a Manager

    On your Google Business Profile, go to Menu › Business Profile settings › People and access › Add, type the HeyFoundly email from your welcome message, choose Manager, and press Invite. No profile yet? We create it and Google sends you a quick verification.

  3. 3 min

    TikTok: share access safely

    TikTok has no "add a manager" button, so we use a secure one-time link we send you. You keep the 2-step code on your phone, so the account always stays under your control.

  4. 3 min

    Send us your shop's details

    Your menu or price list, opening hours, a few photos, and how you like to talk to customers (friendly, formal, fun). Reply to our welcome message with them.

Want us to stop at any time? Remove HeyFoundly from your settings in one click. Questions: reply to your welcome message.
